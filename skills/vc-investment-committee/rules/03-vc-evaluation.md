## Stage 2: 10-Person VC Evaluation

Each of 10 VC personas independently evaluates the opportunity using this format:

```
### [Name] — [Tagline]
**Verdict Tendency**: 🟢 Invest / 🔴 Pass / 🟡 Dig Deeper
**Strengths** (2-3): What this persona rates highly
**Concerns** (2-3): What this persona worries about
**Unique Perspective**: Distinctive angle only they would see (1-2 sentences)
```

### Requirements

- **Complete evaluations for all 10 without omission**
- Each persona must use their distinctive voice, frameworks, and vocabulary
- Verdicts must be grounded in the persona's documented criteria and philosophy
- Unique Perspective should surface something no other persona would catch
