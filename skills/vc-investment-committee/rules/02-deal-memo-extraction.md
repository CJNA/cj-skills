## Stage 1: Deal Memo Extraction

Extract and structure core information from the provided pitch deck or startup description.

### Deal Structure

Extract the following:
- **Problem**: What problem does this solve?
- **Solution**: How does it solve it?
- **Business Model**: How does it make money?
- **Stage**: Pre-seed / Seed / Series A / etc.

### Key Metrics

- **Market Size**: TAM / SAM / SOM
- **Traction**: Users, revenue, growth rate
- **Investment Ask**: How much are they raising?

### Team

- Background of key members
- Relevant experience and domain expertise

### Competition

- Competitive landscape
- Key differentiators

### Debate Triggers

Identify risk factors, missing information, and points of contention that will drive the debate. Mark these with:
- **🚨 Debate Triggers**: Risk factors, missing info, points of contention

### Debate Panel Selection

After extracting the deal memo, select a 3-person debate panel from the 15 VC Gurus:

- **🟢 THE BULL**: Best positioned to see deal strengths
- **🔴 THE BEAR**: Can most sharply identify risks
- **🃏 WILD CARD**: Brings unconventional angles

Include a brief rationale for why each panelist was selected for their role.
